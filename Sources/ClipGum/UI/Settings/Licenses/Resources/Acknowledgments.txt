ClipGum acknowledgments

Sparkle 2 (sparkle-project/Sparkle), pinned to 2.10.0, provides signed appcast/update verification and the updater UI.

Swift Argument Parser (apple/swift-argument-parser), pinned to 1.5.0, provides the standalone CLI's UNIX command and option parsing. Its Apache-2.0 license is included separately.

MLX, mlx-swift, and mlx-swift-lm (ml-explore) provide Apple Silicon local inference primitives. llama.swift (mattt) and llama.cpp (ggml-org) provide the GGUF backend. Their licenses are included separately.

Boop (IvanMathy/Boop) provided a production reference for stateless, supplied-text transformations. Maccy (p0deje/Maccy) provided production references for explicit pasteboard flavor handling, menu-bar behavior, and Sparkle sandbox configuration. ClipGum's marker-first capture and content-firewall policies are independently implemented; no clipboard polling or history behavior was adopted.

PrismML Ternary Bonsai is acknowledged as an optional external model family; no PrismML weights are bundled. See PrismML.txt.

Pure Paste-style minimal-permission and zero-telemetry requirements informed design goals only. Proprietary Pure Paste source was not inspected and no source-level similarity is claimed.
